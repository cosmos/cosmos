# Disclaimers for Fundraiser Participants

**1. Development Risks & Timeline Uncertainty**
The Essential Cosmos Software and Services may not be completed within the **projected timeline** due to unforeseen technical issues or circumstances.
**2. Market Adoption & Competition**
The Cosmos Hub may fail to gain significant market adoption due to competition or a failure of Atom holders to **coordinate** effectively.
**3. Token Dilution Risk**
The first 6 hours of the Fundraiser are uncapped. Consequently, there may be significant token **dilution** relative to the total supply of Atoms at Genesis.
**4. Technical Bugs & Vulnerabilities**
There may be unintended bugs in the software or **protocol design** that cause the project to fail. These include, but are not limited to, bugs in Tendermint Core, the Cosmos Hub ABCI application, or client/wallet software.
**5. Hard-Fork & Network Splitting**
There may be **irreconcilable** differences among Atom holders that could cause the Cosmos Hub blockchain to split (hard-fork) into two or more competing chains. This may result in economic harm to end-users and related services, such as crypto-exchanges.
**6. Governance Risks**
A majority or supermajority of Atom holders may pass proposals or make governance decisions that conflict with the **interests** of the remaining Atom holders.
