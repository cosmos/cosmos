## 1. Entities & Purpose

* **Interchain Foundation (ICF):** A non-profit Swiss Foundation. Its purpose is to conduct a Fundraiser designed to collect donations, to contract with entities and their agents for the development of the Cosmos Essential Software and Services (CESS), and to help foster a community around the CESS.
* **All in Bits, Inc. (AIB):** A for-profit Delaware C-Corp behind Tendermint. The ICF has contracted with AIB to develop the initial portion of the CESS, including Tendermint v1.

## 2. The Fundraiser & Tokenomics

* **The Asset:** ICF is planning a Fundraiser for future staking tokens, called **"atoms"**, that give the holder a limited license to validate the Cosmos Hub and Dex. The contributions made in connection with the Fundraiser will go to the ICF to develop the CESS.
* **Initial Commitments:** The ICF has received several commitments for donations from various individuals that will be tied to a portion of future atoms.
    * **Initial Donors:** Committed an amount equal to 5% of ICF’s initial donation goal.
    * **Strategic Partnerships:** Pre-Fundraiser commitments equal to **$1,329,472** of the ICF fundraising goal.
* **Transparency:** Before the Fundraiser opens, the total number of atoms granted, total donations received, and a pseudonymous list of distinct entities along with their discount rates will be published.

### Timeline & Pricing
* **Launch Date:** Projected for **April 6, 2017 at 6AM PDT**.
* **Duration:** The Fundraiser will last for **2 weeks**.
* **Pricing Model (Day 1):**
    * Suggested donation: **$0.10** per atom.
    * Strategic Funders (25% discount): **$0.075** per atom.
    * Pre-Fundraisers (15% discount): **$0.085** per atom.
* **Vesting:**
    * **AIB's atoms:** Vest over a **period** of two years after Genesis.
    * **Funders' atoms:** No vesting required.

## 3. Distribution & Inflation

* **Post-Fundraiser Allocation:** The total number of atoms distributed is multiplied by 1/3, inflated, and allocated for the ICF, AIB, and Initial Donors.
* **Genesis Distribution:**
    * **ICF:** 10%
    * **AIB:** 10%
    * **Initial Donors:** 5%
    * **Pre-Fundraiser + Fundraiser Donors:** 75%
* **Inflation Logic:**
    * Initially, **7%** of atoms will be inflated annually and distributed to bonded/active atom holders.
    * This rate adjusts over time to incentivize at least **2/3** of the total atoms to be bonded.
    * Inactive/unbonded holders do not earn rewards (effectively taxed by inflation).

## 4. Governance & Network Mechanics

* **Genesis Validator Selection:** Upon completion of the Fundraiser, all atom holders (including ICF and AIB) play the **Delegation Game**. The top 100 validators after delegation will be chosen for Genesis.
* **Validator Responsibilities:**
    * Must participate in governance or risk inactivation/unbonding.
    * Must run the sanctioned consensus protocol without modification to avoid slashing.
    * Must secure servers against hacking.
* **Delegator Responsibilities:** Delegators must choose validators wisely. If a validator is hacked, malicious, or incompetent, the delegator risks losing their delegated atoms.
* **Atom Utility:** Atoms are a tool (like Bitcoin miners), not a medium of exchange or store of value.
* **Unbonding Period:** 1 month.

## 5. Legal Disclaimers & Risk Factors

## Definitions

* **Activate Atoms:** Atoms staked by active validators. Inactive atoms do not earn fees or rewards.
* **Bonded Atoms:** Atoms posted as collateral ("at stake"). These are at risk of slashing.
* **Cosmos Constitution:** A document outlining the social contract of the Cosmos Network.
* **Cosmos Dex:** A distributed exchange zone providing fast matching with distributed custody.
* **Cosmos Hub:** The root zone connecting other zones, facilitating token movement.
* **Cosmos Hub Block Reward:** Transaction fees + Inflationary atoms (initial rate ~20% of total atoms/year implied, 1/5 ratio mentioned in definitions vs 7% in text - *Subject to governance*).
* **Delegator:** An atom holder who assigns validating power to a validator, sharing both reward and risk.
* **Governance:** The process for ratifying proposals (parameter changes, upgrades, etc.).
* **Unbonding Period:** The mandatory waiting period (1 month) to make atoms transferable after bonding.
* **Validator:** Full nodes responsible for committing blocks. Limited to 100 spots at Genesis.
